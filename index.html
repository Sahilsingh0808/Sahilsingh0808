Spine AI (YC S23)Research platform2025 - 26

Spine Swarm

Spine is an AI research platform that runs 400+ models as parallel agents and returns client-ready decks, PDFs and Excel models with formulas. Its deep-research engine ranked #1 on Google DeepMind's DeepSearchQA benchmark at 87.6% accuracy. Investors, consultants, legal, medical and GTM teams use it to turn days of analyst work into a single run.

What I built

  • The Swarm orchestration engine. The backend that coordinates 1 to 10 specialised agents per task, with 500+ running at once, so multi-step research runs in parallel instead of in sequence.
  • The model router. Picks from 400+ LLMs at each step for quality, latency and cost. It has processed 106k+ intelligence nodes.
  • Canvas APIs and data layer. 6,400+ canvas environments with collaborative agent runs, artifact persistence, branching and inspectable intermediate state.
  • The React canvas. The visual canvas UI, scheduled workflows, and integrations over MCP, webhooks and external APIs.
  • Multi-cloud DevOps. AWS, GCP and Azure with zero-downtime blue-green deploys, kept reliable across hundreds of long-running agent tasks.

Why it matters

Most AI research tools return chat. Spine returns deliverables, and chooses a model per step instead of pinning a whole task to one LLM. Swarm is what lets a research task run as a parallel graph and come back as artifacts a person would otherwise spend a day producing.

SigIQ.aiUPSC civil services2024 - 25

Padhai.ai

Padhai.ai is an AI study companion for Indian civil-services aspirants: an AI tutor across the UPSC syllabus, daily current affairs linked to past questions, 8,000+ previous-year questions with model answers and AI evaluation for Mains, and chat over the NCERT and UPSC textbooks. It serves 175,000+ students, with 25M+ tutor interactions and 10M+ AI summaries.

What I built

  • AI webserver refactor. Restructured the codebase and hardened error handling, which made it far more stable under exam-season load.
  • WebSocket service for 10k concurrent sessions. The real-time layer behind tutor chat, with a separate CPU-heavy recordkeeping service so the hot path stays fast.
  • Redis leaderboard. Real-time ranks from sorted sets, with logarithmic-time reads and no load on the database.
  • Blue-green deploys and Datadog APM. Zero-downtime releases on Docker, with centralised logging and request traces across the AI webservers.
  • Growth surfaces. FCM notifications and deeplink sharing with generated preview images.

SigIQ.aiK-12 and GRE2024 - 25

EverTutor.ai

EverTutor gives struggling students personalised, voice-first AI tutoring to reach grade level: 50+ languages, adaptive difficulty, integrations with Zearn, Illustrative Math, Desmos and Waggle, and FERPA and COPPA compliance. It started as a GRE prep product before becoming the K-12 intervention platform it is today.

What I built

  • Backend and infra. The AI webserver, microservices, queues and DevOps, through the move from GRE to K-12.
  • Stripe billing. The full Stripe integration for domestic and international payments and subscriptions.
  • React Native rich text and LaTeX renderer. The runtime that renders every tutor answer: Markdown, LaTeX and custom components for math-heavy responses.
  • Containerised multi-cloud deploys. AWS, GCP and Azure on blue-green releases, with CI/CD tuned for cost and availability and Datadog APM.

Why it matters

One-to-one tutoring is the strongest intervention there is, and cost is what stops it scaling. Voice-first AI gets close at a price schools can pay. Whether such a product survives a real classroom comes down to the backend that keeps payments, content and AI flowing reliably, which is the part I owned.
